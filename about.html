Introduction

Welcome to My Cooking Journey

This website is a personal record of the recipes I make, the cooking techniques I practice, and the lessons I learn through experience.

My Story

I am beginning this project as a developing home cook. I want to become more comfortable preparing meals, following recipes, and understanding how ingredients and cooking methods work together.

Rather than waiting until I become highly experienced, I am documenting the journey from the beginning. This allows me to preserve both sucessful attempts and mistakes that can help me improve.

Why I Created This Website

I created this website to keep my recipes, cooking notes, photographs, and personal reflections organized in one place.

Building the website also gives me an opportunity to learn web development while working on a subject that is personally meaningful to me.

What I Am Learning

My current cooking focus includes:

  • Measuring ingredients accurately
  • Following recipes in the correct sequence
  • Controlling cooking temperatures
  • Using basic kitchen tools safely
  • Improving timing and preparation
  • Learning from unsuccessful attempts

I am also learning how to create this website using:

  • HTML for content and structure
  • CSS for visual design and layout
  • JavaScript for future interactivity
  • Visual Studio for project development

My Cooking Goals

My short-term goals are:

  • Practice a small collection of dependable recipes
  • Become more comfortable working without rushing
  • Understand common cooking terms and techniques
  • Improve consistency between repeated attempts

My long-term goal is to become a confident and adaptable cook who understands both how to prepare a dish and why each step is important.

What You Will Find Here

This website will continue to grow as my cooking and web-development skills improve.

  • Personal recipes
  • Recipe photographs
  • Cooking-journal entries
  • Lessons from mistakes
  • Progress between repeated attempts
  • New website features added over time
