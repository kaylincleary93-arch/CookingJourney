Featured Recipe

Homemade Pancakes

These homade pancakes are one of the first breakfast recipes I am learning to make from scratch.

The goal is to create pancakes that are soft, fluffy, and evenly cooked.

Recipe Summary

  • Preparation time:10 minutes
  • Cooking time: 15 minutes
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Servings 4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Gather and measure all of the ingredients.
  2. Combine the flour, sugar, and baking powder in a large mixing bowl.
  3. Add the milk, egg, and melted butter. Stir until the ingredients are combined.
  4. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour a small amount of batter into the pan.
  6. Cook until bubbles form on the surface, then flip the pancake.
  7. Cook the second side until it is golden brown.
