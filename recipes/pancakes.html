Homemade Pancakes

A fluffy homemade pancake recipe perfect for beginners.

Home Recipes Cooking Journal About

These homemade pancakes are light, fluffy, and easy to prepare using everyday ingredients.

Recipe Information

  • Preparation Time: 10 minutes
  • Cook Time: 15 minutes
  • Servings: 4
  • Difficulty: Beginner

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups milk

Equipment

  • Mixing bowl
  • Whisk
  • Measuring cups
  • Measuring spoons
  • Non-stick skillet
  • Spatula

Instructions

  1. Combine the dry ingredients in a mixing bowl.
  2. Whisk together the wet ingredients.
  3. Mix the wet and dry ingredients until just combined.
  4. Heat a lightly greased skillet over medium heat.
  5. Pour batter onto the skillet.
  6. Cook until bubbles form on the surface.
  7. Flip and cook until golden brown.
  8. Serve warm.

Notes

Avoid overmixing the batter. Small lumps are normal and help produce lighter pancakes.

Tips

  • Preheat the pan before adding batter.
  • Test one pancake before cooking the entire batch.
  • Keep finished pancakes warm in a low-temperature oven.
