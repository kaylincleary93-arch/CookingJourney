My First Homemade Pancakes

August 2, 2026

Today I practiced making homemade pancakes from scratch. I focused on measuring the ingredients carefully, mixing the batter, and cooking each pancake evenly.

What went well: The pancakes were soft, and most of them cooked without burning.

What was difficult: The pancakes were not all the same size, and some cooked faster than others.

Lesson learned: I need to use the same amount of batter for every pancake and monitor the pan temperature more carefully.

My next goal is to create pancakes that are evenly sized and evenly browned.

Practicing Scrambled Eggs

August 3, 2026

Today I practiced making scrambled eggs over low heat. I wanted to learn how slowly cooking the eggs changes their texture.

What went well: The eggs stayed soft and did not stick badly to the pan.

What was difficult: I waited too long to remove the eggs from the heat, so they became firmer than I wanted.

Lesson learned: Food can continue cooking after it is removed from direct heat, so timing matters near the end of the process.

Next time, I will remove the eggs from the heat slightly earlier.
